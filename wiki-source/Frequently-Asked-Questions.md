# Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Enoa-Engine II real?
Yes. It is a proprietary engine and editor in active development at EnoaSoft. This wiki and the public repository are its official public reference.

### Is the source code available?
No. The engine source and binaries remain private, and this wiki does not provide permission to reproduce or redistribute them.

### Can I download Enoa-Engine II or Silent Patrol: Vietnam?
No public engine SDK/editor or game download is announced here.

### Is it a fork of Unreal Engine or an older Vietnam War game?
No. Enoa-Engine II is developed as EnoaSoft's own technology. The same **Silent Patrol: Vietnam** game is being worked on in UE5 and in the proprietary engine.

### What does the LOD comparison prove?
It documents a reduction from 5.23M to 0.82M triangles in a particular development scene; it does not prove a corresponding improvement in native Windows FPS.

### Why are there very low FPS counters in some captures?
The supplied test captures were made via **Wine**. They are not comparable to native Windows GPU benchmark results.

### Can the engine create a standalone game?
The game export workflow and a packaged game's menu have been demonstrated. The team reports testing the exported executable; it is still an actively developed toolchain.

### How can I follow the project?
See the [official public repository](https://github.com/EnoaSoft-s-r-o/enoa-engine-II-public) and [enoasoft.cz](https://enoasoft.cz).
